### 1. Problem summary
Your code fails to upload to your Arduino, resulting in a "programmer is not responding" error message.

### 2. Likely causes and fixes

* **Incorrect Board or Port selected in the IDE**
  * **Why it happens:** The Arduino IDE is trying to talk to the wrong device or an inactive port.
  * **Check:** In the Arduino IDE, open **Tools > Board** and **Tools > Port**.
  * **Fix:** Select your exact Arduino model and the active serial port associated with your board. If using an Arduino Nano, try changing **Tools > Processor** to "ATmega328P (Old Bootloader)".

* **Hardware connected to RX/TX pins (Pins 0 and 1)**
  * **Why it happens:** Digital pins 0 and 1 share the serial line used to upload code. External components (like Bluetooth modules or GPS) block serial communication.
  * **Check:** Look at your Arduino to see if any wires or shields are connected to pins 0 (RX) and 1 (TX).
  * **Fix:** Disconnect all wires from pins 0 and 1 while uploading code, then reconnect them once the upload completes.

* **Power-only USB cable or missing driver**
  * **Why it happens:** Some USB cables only transmit power and no data. Also, board clones often require custom USB drivers (like CH340).
  * **Check:** Unplug and re-plug the board while checking your computer's Device Manager (Windows) or System Information (Mac) to see if a port appears.
  * **Fix:** Swap to a known data-capable USB cable, or download and install the appropriate USB-to-serial driver for your board.

### 4. Safety notes
Always unplug the board from power/USB before changing any wiring to avoid short circuits.
