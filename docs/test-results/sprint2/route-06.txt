### 1. Problem summary
Your character LCD screen turns on, but only displays solid black boxes (usually on the top row) instead of your text.

---

### 2. Likely causes and fixes

*   **Cause 1: Incorrect contrast adjustment**
    *   **Why:** If the contrast voltage on the LCD's contrast pin (V0) is set too high, all pixel blocks light up completely.
    *   **Simple check:** Look for a small blue potentiometer (trimpot) on your breadboard or on the back of the LCD module. Turn it slowly while the board is powered.
    *   **Fix:** Adjust the potentiometer until the solid boxes fade away and text becomes clear.

*   **Cause 2: Incorrect wiring or loose connections**
    *   **Why:** The LCD shows black boxes by default when it receives power, but fails to receive initialization commands from the microcontroller.
    *   **Simple check:** Check if any jumper wires are loose, or if data pins (RS, EN, D4ΓÇôD7) or I2C pins (SDA, SCL) are plugged into the wrong pins.
    *   **Fix:** Re-seat all jumper wires firmly and verify that the physical wiring matches the pin numbers defined in your code.

*   **Cause 3: Wrong I2C address (if using an I2C adapter module)**
    *   **Why:** If your LCD uses a 4-pin I2C adapter, the code will fail to communicate if the programmed I2C address is incorrect.
    *   **Simple check:** Run an "I2C scanner" sketch on your microcontroller to detect the screen's address.
    *   **Fix:** Update the address in your code (common defaults are `0x27` or `0x3F`).
